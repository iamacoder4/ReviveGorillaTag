The RGT Goals

A clear, actionable plan to bring Gorilla Tag back from the brink — for good.

01

Community Events

Regular tournaments, casual lobbies, hide-and-seek events, and themed game nights to bring players back into the fold and keep them engaged week after week.

02

Content Revival

Partner with content creators to flood YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch with fresh Gorilla Tag content — funny moments, speedruns, and gameplay highlights that bring in new eyes.

03

Mod & Custom Content

Support and promote the modding community to extend the game's lifespan with new maps, cosmetics, game modes, and quality-of-life improvements players actually want.

04

Onboarding New Players

Create beginner guides, welcome new VR players into the community, and make the first experience in Gorilla Tag an unforgettable one they'll want to come back to.

05

Toxic-Free Zones

Establish moderated spaces where players of all ages can enjoy the game without fear of harassment. Gorilla Tag is for everyone — full stop.
