ABOUT RGT

What is Revive Gorilla Tag?

We're not just a fan page — we're a grassroots movement built by players, for players.

Gorilla Tag is one of the most iconic VR games ever created — a simple, physics-driven experience where players swing, jump, and tag each other as gorillas. At its peak, it was a cultural phenomenon in the VR space.

But like many games, the flame started to fade. Player counts dropped, the community splintered, and the magic began to disappear. That's where RGT comes in.

Revive Gorilla Tag is a community-led initiative to bring back everything that made Gorilla Tag special — and push it further than ever before.

Community Events Content Creation Player Growth Mod Support Tournaments

The Game

A physics-based VR tag game where you move by pushing off the ground with your hands. Pure gorilla energy.

The Community

Thousands of players who love the game and want to see it thrive again. We are gorillas united.

The Mission

Restore the playerbase, host community events, grow content creators, and keep the spirit alive.

THE SITUATION

Why Does Gorilla Tag Need Revival?

Understanding the problem is the first step to fixing it.

Declining Players

Peak player counts have dropped significantly as newer VR titles pulled attention away from the gorilla arena.

Fragmented Community

Players scattered across platforms, servers, and groups — with no central rally point to unite them.

Fading Content

Content creators moved on, streams dried up, and the hype that once drove millions of views fizzled out.

Unresolved Issues

Persistent bugs, toxic players, and lack of fresh content pushed many long-time fans away from the game.

Sound familiar? We're fixing that.

OUR MISSION

The RGT Goals

A clear plan to bring Gorilla Tag back from the brink.

01

Community Events

Regular tournaments, casual lobbies, hide-and-seek events, and themed game nights to bring players back and keep them engaged.

02

Content Revival

Partner with content creators to flood YouTube, TikTok, and Twitch with fresh Gorilla Tag content — funny moments, speedruns, and highlights.

03

Mod & Custom Content

Support and promote the modding community to extend the game's lifespan with new maps, cosmetics, game modes, and quality-of-life improvements.

04

Onboarding New Players

Create beginner guides, welcome new VR players into the community, and make the first experience in Gorilla Tag unforgettable.

05

Toxic-Free Zones

Establish moderated spaces where players of all ages can enjoy the game without fear of harassment. Gorilla Tag is for everyone.
